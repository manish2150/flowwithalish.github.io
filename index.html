About Caffeol Coffee Collage

Our Coffee Journey in Pokhara

Established in 2018, Caffeol Coffee Collage has become Taxi Chowk's premier destination for coffee enthusiasts and aspiring baristas in Pokhara. Our mission is to provide exceptional coffee experiences while training the next generation of coffee professionals.

We source premium beans from local Nepali growers and international suppliers, roasting them to perfection in-house. From our signature espresso drinks to our specialty pour-overs, every cup is crafted with passion and precision.

Our space is designed as both a welcoming cafe and a training center where coffee lovers can learn, connect, and enjoy the art of coffee. We're proud to be Pokhara's only cafe that combines premium coffee service with professional barista education.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your opening hours?

We are open 7 days a week from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM. On weekends, we extend our hours until 10:00 PM.

Do you offer barista training certifications?

Yes, we offer certified barista training programs at three levels: Basic, Advanced, and Latte Art. All courses include certificates upon completion.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, credit/debit cards, and mobile wallets. For barista courses, we accept installment payments.

Do you have student discounts?

Yes! We offer a 15% discount for students on all coffee and food items. Just show your student ID card when ordering.

Can I reserve a table for studying or meetings?

Yes, you can reserve tables for study groups or meetings by calling us at +977 9801234567. We have dedicated quiet zones for studying.
