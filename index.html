About Flavour House

Our Story in Pokhara

Established in 2015, Flavour Cafe has become Bagar 1's favorite destination for coffee lovers and food enthusiasts in Pokhara. Located near Prithvi Narayan Campus, our mission is to provide a premium cafe experience with exceptional food and beverages in a warm, inviting atmosphere.

We source the finest ingredients locally and prepare each dish with care and attention to detail. From our signature momos to our aromatic teas, every item on our menu is crafted to perfection.

Our cafe is designed to be a welcoming space where students can study, friends can gather, professionals can work, and families can enjoy quality time together over great food and drinks with a view of beautiful Pokhara.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your opening hours?

We are open 7 days a week from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM. On weekends, we extend our hours until 11:00 PM.

Do you offer home delivery in Pokhara?

Yes, we offer home delivery throughout Pokhara, with priority service in Bagar 1 and surrounding areas near Prithvi Narayan Campus. You can place your order through WhatsApp using our "Order Now" feature on each menu item.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash and mobile wallets. For home delivery, we accept cash on delivery and mobile wallets. .

Do you have student discounts?

Yes! We offer a 10% discount for students from Prithvi Narayan Campus and other educational institutions. Just show your student ID card when ordering.

Can I reserve a table in advance?

Yes, you can reserve a table by calling us at +977 9709720243. We recommend reservations for weekends and evenings, especially during exam seasons at the campus.
